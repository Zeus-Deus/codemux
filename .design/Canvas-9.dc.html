Four directions. All four share one row anatomy: a two-line title, then where it ran (project · branch or worktree · agent · when), and every pick states where it will open before anything moves. They differ in where the sessions live in the app.

Keeps today's entry points (row + /resume + the + menu) but fixes the list itself. Sessions group under the folder they ran in; the draft's own project comes first and open. A scope toggle replaces the "Search every project" row. Worktree sessions get a pill instead of being mislabelled "this checkout". Hovering a row answers the open question in the footer.
